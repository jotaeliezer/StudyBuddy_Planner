Privacy Policy

StudyBuddy Planner is a study planner that runs in your browser. We do not create accounts, and we do not collect your study data on a server.

Your planner data (tasks, schedule, settings, and similar) is stored only in your browser using localStorage. Clearing site data or switching browsers/devices removes or does not share that data.

This site may show ads in the future using Google AdSense. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses data for ads.
